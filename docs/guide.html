Playing nidhogg

nidhogg runs Chompi's own TAPE firmware on norns, with an OMX-27 as the panel. TAPE is a sampler and a tape looper: play samples across the keys, record your own, and loop it all on a varispeed tape with delay and reverb. This covers the test build: TAPE only.

Where everything is

E1 is Chompi's Play/Record switch. In Play, AUX is shift. In Record, it records.

K2 and K3 run the looper.

K1 held while you nudge a pot pushes that knob, which steps it to its next page.

Every OMX key lights up with the colour Chompi's own key would show.

Knobs and pages

Each knob has two or three jobs, called pages. Hold K1 and nudge the pot to step to the next page. The screen shows which page you're on as dots.

A pot does nothing until you sweep it past the knob's current setting, then it takes over. Until then the screen shows a small triangle where the pot is, and which way to sweep.

Playing samples

JAMMI mode plays one sample across all the keys. White key 8 plays it at its own pitch. Up to 7 notes sound at once.

CUBBI mode gives each white key its own sample at its own pitch, each with its own knob settings.

To pick a sample in JAMMI, hold AUX and press a white key. Keys 1-14 are the card's slots in the current bank; key 15 is the RAM slot, where your own recordings go. Slots that have a sample light up in the bank's colour while you hold AUX.

After start-up you're in JAMMI on slot 15. Until you record something it plays a built-in test tone.

The shift menu

Hold AUX and the black keys and pots change jobs. Let go to go back.

Pots and pushes while AUX is held

  • Pitch tunes in fifths and fourths. Page 2 is pan.
  • Start and End slide the whole start-to-end window. Page 2 sets attack and release together.
  • Magic sets the delay time (and reverb length), then warble, then filter resonance.
  • Volume sets the output compressor.
  • Push Start: auto-loop on or off. Push End: sustain on or off. Push Magic: reset all FX. Push Volume: where the input is heard.
  • K2 / K3: overdub level down or up.

Recording a sample

1

Turn E1 right for Record. You now hear the input.

2

Hold AUX while you make the sound. Let go to stop. The screen shows SAMPLING.

3

Turn E1 left for Play and play it on the keys. It's in slot 15.

Keep it

Hold AUX, press save, press a white key 1-14 for where it goes, then press AUX to confirm. Copy and erase work the same way.

The input is whatever the shift menu has chosen: mic (norns left input), line (both inputs) or resample (nidhogg itself).

The looper

emptyLOOP → recordingLOOP → overdubLOOP → playingPLAY ⇄ paused
  • LOOP starts the first recording. Press again to close the loop, and it carries straight on overdubbing. Press again to stop overdubbing, and again to start a new layer.
  • PLAY stops and starts the tape, with a little tape-stop slowdown. Hold it 2 seconds while paused to rewind.
  • PLAY + LOOP on an empty looper: recording starts with your next note.
  • Hold PLAY + LOOP for 2 seconds to clear the loop.
  • Transport (OMX encoder or E2) sets the tape speed from -2x to 2x while playing. While paused, turning it scrubs the tape. Push the OMX encoder for normal speed.
